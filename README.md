### 🚀 About Me

I'm a final-year **B.Tech (ECE)** undergrad at Heritage Institute of Technology (CGPA **8.86**), living at the intersection of **Generative AI**, **backend engineering**, and **multi-agent systems**. I like taking AI from "cool demo" to "deployed, observable, production system" — RAG pipelines, LangGraph orchestrators, and scalable FastAPI backends included.

- 🔭 Currently a **GenAI R&D Intern @ Quantyra Analytics**, building agentic assistants for AI-powered marketing tools
- 🧬 Previously fine-tuned open-source LLMs on **Bengali datasets** as an AI Researcher at the **University of Calcutta**
- 🏛️ Built a RAG-based virtual assistant for **Government of West Bengal scheme delivery** during my internship at **Webel**
- 🌱 Currently deepening my skills in **Transformers, fine-tuning (Unsloth/vLLM)**, and multi-agent orchestration with **MCP**
- 💬 Always up for a conversation about agentic AI, RAG architecture, or LLM fine-tuning
